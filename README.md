# AI Research Learning Journey

This repository documents my journey into Artificial Intelligence and AI Research.

My goals are:

- Learn AI fundamentals
- Read and understand research papers
- Build research habits
- Develop critical thinking about AI systems
- Track my progress publicly

## Repository Contents

### Research Notes

Summaries of influential AI papers written in beginner-friendly language.

### Learning Log

Weekly reflections on concepts learned and questions explored.

### Datasets

Interesting datasets that can be useful for future AI projects.

### Resources

Books, courses, papers and educational materials for AI learning.

## Current Interests

- Large Language Models
- Natural Language Processing
- AI Safety
- Machine Learning Fundamentals
- Research Methodology

## Learning Philosophy

Rather than rushing into building complex systems, I aim to first develop a strong understanding of the concepts that drive modern AI research.
