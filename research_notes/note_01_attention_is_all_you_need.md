# Paper Note: Attention Is All You Need

## Authors

Ashish Vaswani et al.

## Why This Paper Matters

This paper introduced the Transformer architecture, which became the foundation of many modern AI systems.

## Main Idea

Instead of processing words one by one, the model uses attention mechanisms to determine which words are most important for understanding context.

## Key Concepts

- Self-attention
- Multi-head attention
- Positional encoding
- Encoder-decoder architecture

## My Understanding

The major innovation is allowing the model to focus on relevant parts of a sentence regardless of position.
