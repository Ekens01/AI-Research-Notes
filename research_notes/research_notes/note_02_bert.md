# Paper Note: BERT

## Authors

Jacob Devlin et al.

## What BERT Does

BERT learns language representations by reading text in both directions.

## Key Contributions

- Bidirectional training
- Masked language modeling
- Fine-tuning for downstream tasks

## Applications

- Search engines
- Question answering
- Text classification

## My Reflection

BERT demonstrated that pretraining on large amounts of text can dramatically improve language understanding.
